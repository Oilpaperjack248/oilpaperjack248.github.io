💡 How It Works

Trackpad-Haptics-Photoshop-Plugin creates a bridge between Photoshop's actions and your trackpad's vibration motor. When you zoom, the plugin sends a signal to your trackpad, generating a specific vibration pattern. For smart guide snapping, it detects when elements align perfectly and triggers a distinct tactile pulse.

🔧 Technical Insight: The plugin uses Windows' built-in haptic feedback capabilities, ensuring broad compatibility with most modern laptops. It runs in the background with minimal resource usage (typically less than 5% CPU and 50MB RAM).

🔧 Troubleshooting & Support

❓ Plugin Not Working?

First, ensure you're running the latest version of Adobe Photoshop. Then restart both Photoshop and the plugin. If issues persist, check that your trackpad drivers are up to date.

❓ No Vibration Feedback?

Check your system's haptic settings. Some laptops require haptics to be enabled in Windows Settings → Devices → Touchpad. Also verify the plugin is running (look for the icon in your system tray).

❓ Too Strong or Too Weak Feedback?

Open the plugin settings from the system tray and adjust the intensity slider. You can also choose from preset profiles: Light, Standard, or Strong vibration patterns.

❓ Compatibility Check

This plugin works best with laptops that have built-in haptic touchpads. If your device doesn't have a vibration motor, the plugin will still display visual indicators on screen.
